## Design Context

### Users

People who process images locally in the browser (compress, crop, EXIF, split, combine, bg-remove, filters, favicon). They want a dense workbench, not a marketing site.

### Brand Personality

- Cold observation room. Opaque chrome. One cyan accent.
- Precise: 6px radius max, tabular spec numbers, no pill chrome.
- Local-first: operational copy, no hero slogans.

### Aesthetic Direction

Locked in repo-root `DESIGN.md` (Imago · 灯箱). Tokens there are the only hex source.

- Room `#12151a`, chrome `#1a1e25`, well `#dce3eb`, accent `#1e7a8a`.
- IBM Plex Sans + Noto Sans SC; IBM Plex Mono for KB / size / ratio.
- Anti-references: mint SaaS, amber CTA, warm-brass 工艺台, Inter, glass, glow orbs, rainbow tool colors, Sparkles.

### Design Principles

1. The well is the work. Chrome stays dark and quiet.
2. One accent. Danger is only for delete.
3. Sheets sit on the well; they do not hover-lift or glow.
4. Empty state: 「把图放到灯箱上」. Drop flashes the well 8% toward white for 180ms.
