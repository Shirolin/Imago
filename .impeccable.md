## Design Context

### Users

People who process images locally in the browser (compress, crop, EXIF, split, combine, bg-remove, filters, favicon). They want a dense workbench, not a marketing site.

### Brand Personality

- Cream canvas. Opaque boards one shade darker. One coral accent.
- Editorial: serif wordmark at 500 / -0.02em, 20px well, 8px controls, tabular spec numbers.
- Local-first: operational copy, no hero slogans. 暖纸 is an internal taste-card name, not chrome.

### Aesthetic Direction

Locked in repo-root `DESIGN.md` (Imago · 暖纸). Tokens there are the only hex source.

- Paper `#FAF9F5`, board `#F5F0E8`, well `#EFE9DE`, ink `#141413`, muted `#6C6A64`, accent `#CC785C`, hairline `#E6DFD8`, product `#181715`.
- Noto Serif SC for the wordmark, 工单, and empty headline; Noto Sans SC for controls and specs.
- Cover first: mounted vertical snapshot + typeset 工单 + 「开始处理」. Workbench second: sidebar + paper well. Sidebar wordmark is Imago only.
- Anti-references: dark-IDE 灯箱 teal, mint SaaS, amber CTA, warm-brass 工艺台, Inter, IBM Plex, glass, glow orbs, rainbow tool colors, Sparkles, `font-black`, uppercase control labels, Claude Design product chrome, 9-tool grids, theme toggle on the cover.

### Design Principles

1. Opening is the cover. The empty well is beat two, after 「开始处理」.
2. One accent. Danger is only for delete. Cover and workbench rooms stay on light paper.
3. List cards are cream-matted prints with a wide margin. Filename under the photo. Specs stay in the inspector. No terracotta crop-frame around the image.
4. Sheets settle onto the well (translateY 8px → 0, 280ms ease-out). 工单 scroll fades at the edges. They do not peel, flash, bounce, or lift on hover.
5. Empty well copy: 「把图放到这张纸上」. Cover never uses that line.
