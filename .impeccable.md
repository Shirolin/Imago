## Design Context

### Users

People who process images locally in the browser (compress, crop, EXIF, split, combine, bg-remove, filters, favicon). They want a dense workbench, not a marketing site.

### Brand Personality

- Cream canvas. Opaque boards one shade darker. One coral accent.
- Editorial: serif wordmark at 500 / -0.02em, 20px well, 8px controls, tabular spec numbers.
- Local-first: operational copy, no hero slogans.

### Aesthetic Direction

Locked in repo-root `DESIGN.md` (Imago · 暖纸). Tokens there are the only hex source.

- Paper `#FAF9F5`, board `#F5F0E8`, well `#EFE9DE`, ink `#141413`, muted `#6C6A64`, accent `#CC785C`, hairline `#E6DFD8`, product `#181715`.
- Noto Serif SC for the wordmark and empty headline; Noto Sans SC for controls and specs.
- Anti-references: dark-IDE 灯箱 teal, mint SaaS, amber CTA, warm-brass 工艺台, Inter, IBM Plex, glass, glow orbs, rainbow tool colors, Sparkles, `font-black`, uppercase control labels, Claude Design product chrome.

### Design Principles

1. The well is the work. Radius and cream stay on the well; the inspector stays dense.
2. One accent. Danger is only for delete.
3. Sheets settle onto the well (translateY 8px → 0, 280ms ease-out). They do not peel, flash, bounce, or lift on hover.
4. Empty state: 「把图放到这张纸上」.
