## Design Context

### Users

Privacy-conscious creators and developers who need fast, local-first image processing (compressing, resizing, cropping) without uploading data to servers. They value speed, precision, and a professional "tool-like" experience.

### Brand Personality

- **Precise**: Tight spacing, 1px borders, pixel-perfect alignment.
- **Transparent**: Heavy use of backdrop blurs and semi-transparent materials for lightness.
- **Efficient**: Minimalist interactions (merged headers, cycling toggles), zero redundancy.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Visual Tone**: Professional, technical, refined (Figma/Raycast/Linear style).
- **Theme**: Neutral background tones, sharp accents, sophisticated material depth.
- **Anti-references**: Clunky Windows legacy UIs, flashy marketing templates, generic AI-generated "slop".

### Design Principles

1. **Vertical Space is Sacred**: Maximize the canvas, minimize the chrome.
2. **Material Depth over Flat Borders**: Use subtle inner glows and layered shadows to define structure.
3. **Reactive Feedback**: Every interaction must feel physical (elasticity, smooth transitions).
4. **Contextual Action**: Controls should be as close to the target object as possible.
