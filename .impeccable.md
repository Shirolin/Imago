## Design Context

### Users

People who process images locally in the browser (compress, crop, EXIF, split, combine, bg-remove, filters, favicon). They want a dense workbench, not a marketing site.

### Brand Personality

- Cream paper room. Opaque boards. One terracotta accent.
- Editorial: serif wordmark, 28px well, 12px controls, tabular spec numbers.
- Local-first: operational copy, no hero slogans.

### Aesthetic Direction

Locked in repo-root `DESIGN.md` (Imago · 暖纸). Tokens there are the only hex source.

- Paper `#F9F8F6`, board `#FFFCFA`, well `#F3F1EC`, ink `#2A2623`, muted `#8B8680`, accent `#C45C32`.
- Noto Serif SC for the wordmark and empty headline; Noto Sans SC for controls and specs.
- Anti-references: dark-IDE 灯箱, mint SaaS, amber CTA, warm-brass 工艺台, Inter, IBM Plex, glass, glow orbs, rainbow tool colors, Sparkles, Claude Design product chrome.

### Design Principles

1. The well is the work. Big radius and cream stay on the well; the inspector stays dense.
2. One accent. Danger is only for delete.
3. Sheets settle onto the well (translateY 8px → 0, 280ms ease-out). They do not peel, flash, or bounce.
4. Empty state: 「把图放到这张纸上」.
