## Design Context

### Users

People who process images locally in the browser (compress, crop, EXIF, split, combine, bg-remove, filters, favicon). They want a dense workbench, not a marketing site.

### Brand Personality

- Cream paper room. Opaque boards. One terracotta accent.
- Editorial: serif wordmark, 28px well, 12px controls, tabular spec numbers.
- Local-first: operational copy, no hero slogans.

### Aesthetic Direction

Locked in repo-root `DESIGN.md` (Imago · 暖纸). Tokens there are the only hex source.

- Paper `#F9F8F6`, board `#FFFCFA`, well `#F3F1EC`, ink `#2A2623`, muted `#8B8680`, accent `#C45C32`.
- Noto Serif SC for the wordmark, 工单, and empty headline; Noto Sans SC for controls and specs.
- Cover first: mounted vertical snapshot + typeset 工单 + 「开始处理」. Workbench second: sidebar + paper well.
- Anti-references: dark-IDE 灯箱, mint SaaS, amber CTA, warm-brass 工艺台, Inter, IBM Plex, glass, glow orbs, rainbow tool colors, Sparkles, Claude Design product chrome, 9-tool grids, theme toggle.

### Design Principles

1. Opening is the cover. The empty well is beat two, after 「开始处理」.
2. One accent. Danger is only for delete. Light paper is locked.
3. List cards are cream-matted prints. Filename under the photo. Specs stay in the inspector.
4. Sheets settle onto the well (translateY 8px → 0, 280ms ease-out). 工单 scroll fades at the edges.
5. Empty well copy: 「把图放到这张纸上」. Cover never uses that line.
